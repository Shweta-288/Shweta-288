## About Me

I spent five years as an Android Developer, starting at startups in Jaipur and then at Ernst & Young in Bengaluru, working on a mobile product used by over 100 million people. In 2023 I moved into business analysis at EY, and since November 2024 I've been a Business Analyst at The UniQue Culture, an EdTech company. Along the way I completed the BCS Foundation Certificate in Business Analysis and the BCS Practitioner Certificate in Requirements Engineering.

What I bring to a BA role that most BAs don't: I can read an API spec, understand what a requirement means for a mobile architecture, and have that conversation with an engineering team directly, no translation needed in either direction.
